Visual language

↓ / → = a lineage continues through time. A fork = descendants diverge. A contribution / + = one lineage participates in endosymbiosis while the source lineage continues independently. Learn More is available on every major timeline card.

Time perspective

If Earth’s 4.54-billion-year history were compressed into one 24-hour day, Homo sapiens would arrive only in the final few seconds. The timeline is intentionally not drawn to physical scale; otherwise the human section would be nearly invisible.

How to read the dates

~ means approximate. A range reflects scientific uncertainty. ? marks a highly uncertain or hypothetical stage. Dates for deep evolutionary events often come from molecular clocks plus fossils and can change as evidence improves.
